# Week plan, 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-17: the Fable reset week

The session layout and the coordinator prompts for the usage week of Tuesday 2026-11-10 12:00 to Tuesday
2026-11-17 12:00 PST (D225). Each session's coordinator reads its own row and prompt here, nothing else of this
file. The log entries of the week record what happened; this file is not updated with results, only with a
changed plan (targets or themes the user moves at a review). **Replanned at the review of Thursday 2026-11-12
12:30** (§1 "The review"): sessions 1-3 are as they ran; sessions 4-14 are new.

## 1. The budget

- The user spends one Fable usage reset this week. Fable is capped at 50 % of the weekly limit; the reset
  subtracts the Fable usage from the meter (it does not zero it).
- **Phase A, sessions 1-3 (run):** coordinator and reasoning builders on Fable. Fable reached its cap at 03:05
  on Thursday, in session 3, a session and a half early; the reset was applied at 08:15.
- **Phase B, sessions 4-14:** coordinator and builders on Opus, landers and mechanical work on Sonnet. The meter
  read **17 at the review**: 82 points to spend by Tuesday 11:30, about 8 a session.
- **Fable in phase B is not refunded and is rationed: 6 points for the rest of the week**, builders only, never
  the coordinator, and only for the items §2 names. One Fable builder costs about 1.2 points (measured: 3.5
  points per million Fable builder tokens, 250-500k tokens a builder).
- Subscription only all week: no agy builders, no API-key builders, no Batch API round.

**The review (2026-11-12): what phase A measured, and what it changes.**

1. **Why the cap came early.** The floors were set on the whole meter while the cap is on Fable's share, and a
   floor does not stop a run: session 2 passed its floor (27) by 8 points, session 3 its floor (45) by 19.
   Phase B's prompts give a floor **and** a cap (`pacing.py --cap`), and the coordinator is never on Fable, so
   a Fable limit can no longer stop a run.
2. **Opus gains about four times the pairs per point** on files of a known pattern, and the layouts that
   needed Fable are done but one (b-9, sclient by run; a-L7 is on wt/L18). The queue's 404 open rows (3,168
   functions) are mostly rows with the client's names (225 rows, 1,963 functions): Opus work.
3. **Phase B's old risk is gone, its opposite appears.** 82 points over about 98 session hours is 0.84 an
   hour: the coordinator and three Opus builders (the fit: 0.6 + 0.1 per builder; measured 1.0 with two to
   four). Five or six builders the whole time would overspend by Monday, so each session has a cap.
4. **Stuck functions on Fable are the week's worst buy** (8 pairs a point). The leftover packets W6 and W8-W14
   are parked, not rerun. Fable goes to levers that many functions share, not to single functions.
5. **The library region needs no session:** of 4,355 open entries 3,358 were not functions (fixed in the
   metric that night) and 18 functions of source work exist (15 done or ruled out). What is left is two
   crediting rules for the user (P-C1-1 about 110 pairs, P-C1-2 about 240) and two report items: one tools
   builder, not session 11.
6. **Session 4's items are redistributed:** the kept branches and the evidence-backed renames go to a short
   Opus session this afternoon while the tree is quiet; the Opus queue is built there too; the last drafts
   and the quality of new files ride along with every later session.
