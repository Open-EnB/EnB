Residue (after the float fix of UnknownB01A40Type's zeros, committed): two inline-budget effects
and their register/slot consequences, nothing else:
  1. The client inlines UnknownB00A00Type's user copy constructor (3 loads, 3 stores through the
     member's base register) inside the inlined DeltaTemplateClass<UnknownB00A00Type> constructors of
     SameGuildTagColor (54th member) and OtherGuildTagColor (55th); ours calls it out of line there
     (the extra EH states 0x72/0x74, the extra frame slot at [esp+0x14], the 3-dword temporary at
     [esp+0x28] instead of [esp+0x24], ebp/edi holding BaseStats/CurrentStats instead of the colours).
     Light 0x6F9870 (exact) shows the same copy constructor inlined: the client has the user copy.
  2. The client inlines the narrow Add (0x6F8C70) at 4 body sites (Name, Owner, Title, Rank) and calls it
     from EnergyPercent on; ours inlines 5.

What the probes established (tools/vc6/tryv2.py / .work/f3/tryv3.py, one compile each):
  - trivial/pod copy (no user copy ctor): the copy becomes a POD block copy with a different form
    (lea + interleaved stores) and loses Light 0x6F9870 and 0x706DF0: the user copy ctor stays.
  - The copy IS inlined when the colour members are declared anywhere up to the 35th member
    (after Rank, Acceleration, IsRescueBeaconActive, TargetThreatLevel, WarpTriggerTime; each then
    costs about one Add of budget: 4 -> 2 inlined Adds) and refused from the 36th on (after Shake,
    whose constructor is inlined with its 4 members; after Inventory): a nested (depth-2) expansion is
    admitted only while the function's accumulated expansion is under a threshold; depth-1 Adds
    keep being admitted afterwards.
  - The budget is function-wide and grows with the function's own IL: a dead `Children.size()` at the
    END of the body adds one inlined Add (at the start: no change; it spends what it adds); removing
    10 Adds removes 4 inlined Adds; an empty body still refuses the copies; 6/12/20 dead reads at the
    end give 7/10/1 inlined Adds and at 20 the first copy is admitted (every dead read is then called
    out of line, D40): not a source form.
  - No change: the constructor non-inline; the narrow Add defined in-class; `Add(&Name)` through the
    1-arg wrapper (+1 Add); casts on the Add arguments; G_ASSERT as `if`/`do-while`; `!item` in the
    array constructor; a dead read in the narrow Add (-1 Add but size() called out of line).
  - Making Shake's constructor non-inline admits the copies (and 8 Adds) only because Ship shrinks:
    the client inlines Shake (its vtable store is at 0x6F6D94, inside Ship).
Best explanation: at the colour sites the client has more budget headroom than ours with identical
bytes before them, so the client's Ship (or the inlined nested constructors Shake, Inventory, Legos,
Stats) carries more own IL that emits no code, or spends less (a leaner IL form of something inlined
before the 54th member). Not found in 14 forms. Next lead: compare with the Player object's class at
0x751BE0 (it inlines Shake too) once it is in the tree, and look for byte-neutral IL in
DeltaShipInvClass/DeltaStatsClass (the biggest inlined bodies before the colours).
