# Batch 6 hypothesis, before compilation

First06 shows an internal inline helper's local static remains in the same
BSS contribution, in the wrong relative order. Retail references establish
that the PermStringInit preceding section buffer is from kv_pair.cpp, while
the PermStringInit between buffers is from kv_util.cpp. A normal externally
linked inline helper can emit its local static as a common external symbol,
as observed in the value-buffer wrapper. Test that genuine linkage variant;
examine the COFF section and selection, original symbol pairs and code. A
common symbol alone does not prove its position in the final retail link.
