## Prioritized leads sent for focused compiler tests

1. **D8, real null-left RefCountPtr helper.** On retained1 construction_local, replace `if (frame)` with `if (0 != frame)` while keeping the same RefCountPtr, cleanup scope, virtual call and scalar logic. `wwlib/ref_ptr.h` defines this actual null-left overload; `app/client/client_lego_impl.cpp:172,860` documents that the same overload is required to resolve a register swap in the exact Init. Its `FAIL_IF(0 != dummy)` stays enabled and may fold with literal zero. Retail holds level in EBP and fallback 8 in EDI at 0x60DCBF–0x60DD49; current construction_local reverses them, and the target is otherwise 1177 bytes / 369 rows / 10 masked groups. This helper is distinct from construction_frame_local's Peek binding, which is byte-identical to construction_local, and from moving the fallback initialization before the call, which mismatches retail instruction order and gives 11 groups. Inspect full generated control flow, cleanup, assertion, and pair sets; a source change does not establish byte or behavioral equivalence without those checks.

2. **E6, existing accessor at genuine pointer local.** Rebase only dest_clock's two E6 edits onto retained1 and initialize the client pointer using `(ClientClass *)Get_Client()` instead of `(ClientClass *)Client`; preserve the single clock read after the 0x59E600 callback. The existing accessor returns Client, and exact 0x60F1A0 uses it for its related clock expressions. At retail 0x60E899–0x60E8A9, ECX carries the client and EAX receives ESP before the zero-extended clock load; dest_clock instead holds client in EAX and schedules `mov eax,esp` later. Both are 1176 bytes with five groups of residue. Direct accessor use was tested on str_fallback and gave 22 groups; accessor inside the local combined with destination-reference fallback was not tested. Named Duration, explicit 64-bit cast and clock reference on dest_clock all duplicate it. This is an interaction hypothesis, not evidence that the accessor will improve it; stop on a duplicate or worse result.

The Create_Get 0x474080 / 0x47D2E0 comparison in E6 is not, by itself, a confirmed target defect: coordinator independently compared the two 21-byte raw bodies and found them identical with no calls, global accesses, branches or relocations. A masked match still requires a fresh complete relocation, addend and identity audit. D8's Create_NEW experiment must not be kept: it duplicates target bytes but replaces the existing 0x51C600 Create_Get symbol match with Create_NEW and gains 0x47D2E0, so the symbol sets do not preserve.

## Semantics and disposition

Construction_local reads RObj after the constructor and before publishing InterruptInfo, as the retail 0x60DB5D–69 does; its exception and possible alias effects still need final audit before adoption. The E6 fallback destination reference and explicit assign/strlen reproduce the displayed fallback operation order, but whole-function read order, calls, EH cleanup and strings still require auditing. Baseline NOT EXACT source is a reconstruction scaffold, not semantic gold; retain retail failure paths, stores, callbacks, x87 order and rounding. The group review is complete: neither focused test produced a new supported lead, so hand the unmatched functions and their actual evidence to the required independent Sol reviews. This is bounded exhaustion, not a claim that all ordinary source forms are exhausted or a D271 parking decision. Reopen on concrete new helper, source lifetime, or retail evidence rather than a model change or spelling grid.
