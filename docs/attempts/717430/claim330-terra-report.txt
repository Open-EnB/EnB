Historical ledger contains one partial form, source unavailable; stash list empty.
Client calls float Cast for MaxTurnRate and MaxTiltRate; baseline inlines both.
The client uses fchs before multiplying a positive scale for both rates, whereas
baseline folds the sign into the scale. The fixed-speed path precedes each lookup
in client code; baseline shares part of the two lookup branches. Eight initial

Candidate batches, subject to gate: (1) fixed-speed branch order, preserving
failure and count behavior; (2) explicit negative multiplication in the two rate
expressions to recover fchs without an intermediate float store; (3) real local
declaration and initialisation grouping, comparing pointer/store placement.

ui_experience.cpp:132 and ui_cpit.cpp:698 but no specific evidence of its presence
here; any trial is exploratory and needs full lifetime and pair audit. No dead
read or shared header modification is authorised by the brief.
