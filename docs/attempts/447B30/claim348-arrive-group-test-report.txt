All four emitted Arrive raw and masked SHA-256 `1f5b1df2374436c342d05d6e6e56612c9725e89c42d04dce78f5d493482c3258`, identical to baseline and af08: 185 executable bytes, nine masked difference rows versus 196 retail bytes. No result is a zero-mask candidate; full literal, relocation and EH audits therefore were not applicable.
