## Independent residue interpretation

The retail tails do not uniquely prove the old marker's claim of "a second
local." Optimized stack-slot identity permits another meaningful interpretation:
the final subtraction updates the existing scaled vector in place. Pursue's
retail Y store goes back to the scaled Y slot; its Z store goes back to the
scaled Z slot after accounting for the intervening pop esi. Arrive's retail Y
and Z stores similarly use the scaled vector's slots (-0x14 and -0x10). The
baseline/copy variants instead store the final X/Z in the other vector's area.
This is evidence for testing an actual compound arithmetic operator, not proof
that its spelling is original. Normal slot coalescing could also explain it.

Pursue pf02 still has the best valid whole prefix and nine-row final residue;
pf13's nine rows represent another route, not a gain. Arrive af01's seven rows
remain invalid due to changed association. All nine changed-output af forms
